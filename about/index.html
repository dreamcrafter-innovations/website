About DreamCrafter

DreamCrafter Innovations LLC is a Texas limited liability company, solo-founded and operated by a professional software engineer with a multi-year track record building production applications.

Rather than building one large product, DreamCrafter builds many small, focused apps — each aimed at a specific, underserved problem, built on a consistent React Native / Expo stack. Some apps run entirely on-device; others connect to the cloud where that's what the job needs — the portfolio isn't limited to one architecture, just to doing one thing well per app.

The portfolio spans seven categories — gaming, finance, education, family & kids, lifestyle, utility & safety, and sports — in stages ranging from early concept to store-ready. Ruse, a pass-the-phone social deduction game, is furthest along and launching soon on the App Store and Google Play. The full list is on the Apps page.

Each app ships with its own privacy policy, since what data an app collects — if any — differs app to app: some are fully on-device with nothing to disclose, others use Firebase for basic analytics and crash reporting, and a few request specific device permissions (microphone, location) tied directly to what the app does.
