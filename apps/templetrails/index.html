Temple Trails

Temple Trails is an offline-first Hindu temple discovery and storytelling app. India ships first, covering 45 historic temples across 16 states and union territories, with a personal visited/favorites journal — no account or cloud sync required.

What it does

  • 45 historic temples across 16 Indian states and UTs at launch
  • Personal visited/favorites journal, stored on-device
  • USA and other countries planned next on the same data model
  • Available on iOS, Android, and web
