HiveWarden

HiveWarden is a compliance-aware hive-management app for backyard beekeepers and sideliners, built around inspection tracking and inspector-ready reporting.

What it does

  • Hive-by-hive inspection log built for state compliance record-keeping
  • One-tap inspector packet PDF export
  • Fully offline, no hive-count limit, no account required
