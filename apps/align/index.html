Align

Align is a quiz-driven political ideology compass for India — not a top-3 match, but a full field of markers so you can see exactly where you land, issue by issue.

What it does

  • Places you on 17 policy rulers next to national parties (BJP, INC, RSS, CPI/CPI(M)) and 16 regional modules
  • Overall ruler plus 2-D compass views (Economic × Cultural, Economic × Uniformity/Pluralism)
  • Methodology page with adjustable salience weights; shareable text result card; fully offline
