RoastYield

RoastYield is roast-loss-aware costing and lot traceability for micro-roasters — built for pricing bags off what actually comes out of the roaster, not what the green beans cost.

What it does

  • Roast-loss-aware costing so margins reflect actual roasted-weight yield
  • Lot traceability from green beans through roast to sale
  • Built for micro-roasters too small for Cropster, RoastLog, or FlexiBake
