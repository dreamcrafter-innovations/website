Whistle Watch

Whistle Watch uses your microphone to detect pressure-cooker whistles in real time and counts them toward a target you set, so you don't have to hover in the kitchen. Adjustable sensitivity and a custom-training mode tune it to your specific cooker.

What it does

  • Real-time whistle detection with a configurable target count (1–20)
  • Custom whistle training to tune sensitivity to your own cooker
  • Haptic and visual alerts when your target is reached
  • Available on iOS, Android, and web
