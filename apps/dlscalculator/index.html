DLS Calculator

DLS Calculator implements the official ICC Duckworth-Lewis (D/L) Standard Edition method for revising targets in rain-interrupted T20 and ODI cricket matches — enter each team's innings and any stoppages to get an accurate revised target.

What it does

  • Official ICC D/L Standard Edition calculation method
  • Covers both T20 and ODI match formats
