Nightfall: Secret Roles

Known internally as Bhoot Gaon (भूत गाँव) — a spooky, kid-friendly Werewolf/Mafia-style party game set in a haunted Indian village. 4–16 players share one phone, take on secret roles, and try to survive the night through deception and deduction.

What it does

  • 4–16 players, one shared phone
  • Secret-roles deception gameplay in a haunted-village setting
  • Same genre as Ruse, with its own visual branding
