Little Keeps

Little Keeps is a private, offline growth journal for parents. Track your child's milestones and growth journey across multiple profiles, with everything stored on-device — no account, no cloud sync required.

What it does

  • Multi-profile, works across iOS, Android, and web
  • Fully offline — your family's data stays on your device
