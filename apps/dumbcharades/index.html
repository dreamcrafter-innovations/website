Dumb Charades India

One player secretly sees a movie title and has 60 seconds to act it out — no talking, no humming, no mouthing words — while their team shouts guesses. Two teams, dramatic reveals, and movie packs in multiple languages.

What it does

  • 60-second rounds, two teams, unlimited movie titles
  • Movie packs in English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, and more
  • Available on iOS, Android, and web
