Safety 101

Safety 101 is an offline-first emergency reference guide covering the US (50 states + DC) and India (28 states + 8 union territories). A country switcher swaps emergency numbers, services, disaster info, and state lists, while shared first-aid guides automatically substitute the right local numbers.

What it does

  • Covers the US and India, with a one-tap country switcher
  • First-aid guides adapt local emergency numbers automatically
  • Works fully offline — no signal required in an emergency
