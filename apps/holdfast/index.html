Holdfast

Holdfast is a quarantine, tank-cycling, and new-arrival health protocol tracker for freshwater, marine, coral, planted-tank, and koi/pond keepers — built around a rule-based symptom triage engine, entirely offline.

What it does

  • Offline symptom triage: observed signs plus species return a ranked shortlist of likely conditions
  • Treatment conflict warnings, day-by-day quarantine and tank-cycling checklists, and a merged "Today" worklist
  • Water-test log, symptom and incident logs with photos, and a shareable clearance-card export
