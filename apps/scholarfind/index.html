ScholarFind

ScholarFind is a US scholarship-discovery app that works completely offline. It matches students from grade 4 through college to real scholarships by grade, gender, citizenship, state, and more — zero spam, zero application fees, every entry dated and sourced. The full dataset ships inside the app; there's no account and no backend.

What it does

  • Entirely offline — no account, no backend
  • Filters by grade, citizenship, state, and more
  • Every scholarship entry is dated and sourced
