Ruse

One player is secretly the Trickster — they only see the category, not the secret word. Everyone else gives clues, tries to catch the liar, and votes each round. Built for 3–10 players sharing one phone: no Wi-Fi, no signup, no accounts. Just pass the phone and play.

What it does

  • 3–10 players, one shared phone
  • No Wi-Fi, no signup — works fully offline
  • Fast round times, built for party nights and game groups
  • Available on iOS, Android, and web
