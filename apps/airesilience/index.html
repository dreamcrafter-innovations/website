AI Resilience

AI Resilience keeps a small business's recurring obligations in one register — licences and permits, insurance renewals, equipment inspections, staff access, suppliers and tech — so nothing lapses unnoticed.

What it does

  • Starter packs for restaurants, salons, auto repair, retail, contractors and households
  • Renewal timeline with an overdue / never-checked / due-soon view
  • Reminders 30, 7 and 1 days before, and on the day; works fully offline
  • Optional account for backup and email reminders to a teammate
  • Optional AI setup that suggests items for your business; nothing is added until you confirm
  • Available on iOS, Android, and web
