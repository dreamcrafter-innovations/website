Repairability Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This policy describes how Repairability, developed by DreamCrafter Innovations LLC ("we", "us", "our"), handles data. It supplements our general privacy policy, which covers the website and practices common to all of our apps.

What Repairability collects and how it's used

  • Nothing. The whole product catalogue and the scoring logic are built into the app, and every score is calculated on your device. The app works fully offline.
  • It stores two display preferences on your device: light/dark theme and your US/EU market filter.
  • Does not require an account, does not request any device permissions, and does not collect personal information.

Third-party services

None. Repairability does not use Firebase, analytics, advertising, or crash reporting.

Each product lists the retailer and manufacturer pages its prices came from. If you tap one of those links, it opens in your browser and that website's own privacy policy applies. The app does not send anything to those sites itself.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Repairability does not transmit any data off your device. If you use the Share button, the text of that score is handed to your device's share sheet, and you choose where it goes.

Your choices

  • You can stop using Repairability at any time, including uninstalling it, which removes the preferences stored on your device.
  • You can request support or ask questions at contactus@dreamcrafterinnovations.com.

Children

Repairability is intended for general audiences. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page.
