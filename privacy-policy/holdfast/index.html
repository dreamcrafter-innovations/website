Holdfast Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This policy describes how Holdfast, developed by DreamCrafter Innovations LLC ("we", "us", "our"), handles data. It supplements our general privacy policy, which covers the website and practices common to all of our apps.

What Holdfast collects and how it's used

  • Stores tank and arrival records, symptom logs, water-test readings, and any photos you add locally on your device.
  • All symptom triage and treatment-conflict logic runs entirely on-device — no data tables or arithmetic are sent anywhere.
  • Does not require an account and does not collect personal information.

Third-party services

None. Holdfast does not use Firebase, analytics, or crash reporting.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Holdfast does not transmit your data anywhere off your device except when you explicitly export or share a clearance card.

Your choices

  • You can stop using Holdfast at any time, including uninstalling it, which removes any data stored locally on your device.
  • You can request support or ask questions at contactus@dreamcrafterinnovations.com.

Children

Holdfast is intended for general audiences. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page.
