RoastYield Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This policy describes how RoastYield, developed by DreamCrafter Innovations LLC ("we", "us", "our"), handles data. It supplements our general privacy policy, which covers the website and practices common to all of our apps.

What RoastYield collects and how it's used

  • Stores roast batches, costing figures, and lot records locally on your device.
  • Does not require an account and does not collect personal information.

Third-party services

None. RoastYield does not use Firebase, analytics, or crash reporting.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. RoastYield does not transmit your business data anywhere off your device.

Your choices

  • You can stop using RoastYield at any time, including uninstalling it, which removes any data stored locally on your device.
  • You can request support or ask questions at contactus@dreamcrafterinnovations.com.

Children

RoastYield is intended for general audiences. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page.
