Little Keeps Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This policy describes how Little Keeps, developed by DreamCrafter Innovations LLC ("we", "us", "our"), handles data. It supplements our general privacy policy, which covers the website and practices common to all of our apps.

What Little Keeps collects and how it's used

  • Stores notes, photos, and milestones you enter about a child locally on your device.
  • Does not use third-party advertising or ad-tracking SDKs.
  • Offers optional backup. It is off unless you turn it on; when on, your journal (including photos and audio) is copied to your own iCloud account on iOS or your own Google Drive app-data folder on Android. We never receive that copy; it is handled under Apple's or Google's terms.
  • Intended for use by parents/guardians recording a child's growth, not for direct use by children; no data is knowingly collected directly from a child.

Third-party services

Little Keeps does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Any data processed by third-party services listed above is handled under their own policies, solely to operate and improve Little Keeps.

Your choices

  • You can stop using Little Keeps at any time, including uninstalling it, which removes any data stored locally on your device.
  • You can request support or ask questions at contactus@dreamcrafterinnovations.com.

Children

Little Keeps is intended for use by parents or guardians recording a child's growth, not for direct use by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page.
