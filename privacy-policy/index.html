1. Information we collect (general)

  • On-device profile and settings data for products that support local personalization.
  • Aggregate analytics events (for example: app installs/first opens, screen views, and feature-link taps) to understand usage and improve quality.
  • Crash and diagnostics data to monitor reliability and fix technical issues.
  • Technical identifiers provided by platforms/services (for example, app instance identifiers or device-level identifiers exposed by analytics/crash systems).

App-specific detail — including which apps use Firebase, which request device permissions like microphone or location, and which are fully offline with no data collection at all — is in each app's own policy linked above.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and maintain our Services.
  • To improve performance, security, and user experience.
  • To troubleshoot bugs and prevent abuse.

3. Third-party services

Some apps use third-party providers such as Firebase (Google) for analytics, crash reporting, or authentication. These providers may process technical data under their own policies. See each app's individual policy for which services it uses.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited data with service providers only as needed to operate, secure, and improve our Services.

5. Data retention

Retention varies by product and data type. Locally stored preferences generally remain on-device until removed by the user. Service-side analytics and diagnostics retention follows provider and operational settings.

6. Children

Our Services are intended for general audiences unless a specific app's own policy states otherwise. Little Keeps' policy includes additional detail on this, since it involves recording information about a child.

7. Your choices

  • You may stop using a Service at any time, including uninstalling mobile apps.
  • You may request support at contactus@dreamcrafterinnovations.com.

8. International processing

Data may be processed in countries other than your own depending on infrastructure and provider locations.
