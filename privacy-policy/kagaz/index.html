Kagaz Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This policy describes how Kagaz, developed by DreamCrafter Innovations LLC ("we", "us", "our"), handles data. It supplements our general privacy policy, which covers the website and practices common to all of our apps.

What Kagaz collects and how it's used

  • Stores the documents and dates you record locally on your device, used only to calculate your own deadlines.
  • May offer an optional in-app purchase, processed entirely by Apple's or Google's billing systems — we don't receive or store your payment details.
  • Does not require an account and does not collect personal information.

Third-party services

Apple App Store / Google Play billing, for the optional in-app purchase only. Kagaz does not use Firebase, analytics, or crash reporting.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Kagaz does not transmit your document data anywhere off your device.

Your choices

  • You can stop using Kagaz at any time, including uninstalling it, which removes any data stored locally on your device.
  • You can request support or ask questions at contactus@dreamcrafterinnovations.com.

Children

Kagaz is intended for general audiences. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page.
