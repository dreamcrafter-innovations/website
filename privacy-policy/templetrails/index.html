Temple Trails Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

This policy describes how Temple Trails, developed by DreamCrafter Innovations LLC ("we", "us", "our"), handles data. It supplements our general privacy policy, which covers the website and practices common to all of our apps.

What Temple Trails collects and how it's used

  • May request location access to provide directions and nearby-temple information.
  • Visit logs and notes are stored locally on your device.

Third-party services

Temple Trails does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Any data processed by third-party services listed above is handled under their own policies, solely to operate and improve Temple Trails.

Your choices

  • You can stop using Temple Trails at any time, including uninstalling it, which removes any data stored locally on your device.
  • You can request support or ask questions at contactus@dreamcrafterinnovations.com.

Children

Temple Trails is intended for general audiences. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page.
