RigKeeper Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This policy describes how RigKeeper, developed by DreamCrafter Innovations LLC ("we", "us", "our"), handles data. It supplements our general privacy policy, which covers the website and practices common to all of our apps.

What RigKeeper collects and how it's used

  • Stores maintenance records, costs, and documents you add locally on your device.
  • May offer an optional in-app purchase, processed entirely by Apple's or Google's billing systems — we don't receive or store your payment details.
  • Does not require an account and does not collect personal information.

Third-party services

Apple App Store / Google Play billing, for the optional in-app purchase only. RigKeeper does not use Firebase, analytics, or crash reporting.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. RigKeeper does not transmit your data anywhere off your device except when you explicitly export a warranty-claim packet.

Your choices

  • You can stop using RigKeeper at any time, including uninstalling it, which removes any data stored locally on your device.
  • You can request support or ask questions at contactus@dreamcrafterinnovations.com.

Children

RigKeeper is intended for general audiences. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page.
