Align Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This policy describes how Align, developed by DreamCrafter Innovations LLC ("we", "us", "our"), handles data. It supplements our general privacy policy, which covers the website and practices common to all of our apps.

What Align collects and how it's used

  • Stores your quiz responses and computed scores locally on your device, for your own reference and to enable retakes and comparisons.
  • The optional compare feature shares only what you choose to share, directly between devices — no server is involved.
  • Does not require an account and does not collect personal information.

Third-party services

None. Align does not use Firebase, analytics, or crash reporting.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Align does not transmit your quiz data anywhere off your device except when you explicitly choose to share a result.

Your choices

  • You can stop using Align at any time, including uninstalling it, which removes any data stored locally on your device.
  • You can request support or ask questions at contactus@dreamcrafterinnovations.com.

Children

Align is intended for general audiences. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page.
