Puja Checklist Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

This policy describes how Puja Checklist, developed by DreamCrafter Innovations LLC ("we", "us", "our"), handles data. It supplements our general privacy policy, which covers the website and practices common to all of our apps.

What Puja Checklist collects and how it's used

  • Checklist and preparation data you enter is stored locally on your device.
  • No account is required, and no third-party analytics SDKs are used beyond standard platform crash reporting where enabled.

Third-party services

Puja Checklist does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Any data processed by third-party services listed above is handled under their own policies, solely to operate and improve Puja Checklist.

Your choices

  • You can stop using Puja Checklist at any time, including uninstalling it, which removes any data stored locally on your device.
  • You can request support or ask questions at contactus@dreamcrafterinnovations.com.

Children

Puja Checklist is intended for general audiences. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page.
