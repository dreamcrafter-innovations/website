Ruse Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026 (no cross-app tracking; tracking prompt removed)

This policy describes how Ruse, developed by DreamCrafter Innovations LLC ("we", "us", "our"), handles data. It supplements our general privacy policy, which covers the website and practices common to all of our apps.

What Ruse collects and how it's used

  • Uses Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Firebase Realtime Database for aggregate usage analytics, crash diagnostics, and syncing non-personal game-configuration data.
  • Does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Advertising-ID collection is turned off, so the app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission on iOS.
  • Player profiles, scores, and streaks are stored locally on your device.
  • No account is required to play.

Third-party services

Firebase (Google) for analytics, crash reporting, and/or authentication as noted above.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Any data processed by third-party services listed above is handled under their own policies, solely to operate and improve Ruse.

Your choices

  • You can stop using Ruse at any time, including uninstalling it, which removes any data stored locally on your device.
  • You can request support or ask questions at contactus@dreamcrafterinnovations.com.

Children

Ruse is intended for general audiences. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page.
